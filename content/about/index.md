Hi I'm the creator of these posts. If you want to know some about me read this _short bio_.

My real name is **Floriane REMY**. I'm a French doctor (PhD) in biological anthropology, specializing in the study of craniofacial growth and its relationship with orofacial function (sucking/swallowing, chewing, and breathing).  
I have defended my thesis in 2020 and I am currently working as a postdoctoral researcher at the Catholic University of Louvain, in Brussels (Belgium).  
Even though I would be please to tell me more about my own researches, the purpose of this website is to showcase the studies performed by other scholars coming from diverse scientific disciplines to a wider audience, through accessible science communication. Please, learn, enjoy and share!
